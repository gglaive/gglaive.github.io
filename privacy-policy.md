Data We Collect

Account & Identity

Ads watched, surveys completed, loot boxes opened

You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings.

How We Use Your Data

To create and manage your account
To credit and track your Attention Coins earnings
To process withdrawal requests via PayPal
To display your rank and username on the leaderboard
To prevent fraud and abuse
To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Data Storage & Security
Your data is stored securely on Supabase infrastructure (hosted in the EU — Frankfurt, Germany). We use encrypted connections (TLS), row-level security, and access controls.

Data Retention

Account data is retained as long as your account is active
You can delete your account at any time from your Profile page — all data is permanently deleted immediately
Transaction logs may be retained for up to 12 months for fraud prevention
